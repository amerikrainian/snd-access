# Credits

SnD Access is built on [Guildrun Access](https://github.com/amerikrainian/guildrun-access): its buffers, key help, and installer came over nearly whole. Through it, thank you to Chaosbringer216 and his wonderful work on [Say the Spire 2](https://github.com/bradjrenshaw/say-the-spire2), where the buffer model and the arrangeable announcements were lifted from (with his permission).

Speech goes through [Prism](https://github.com/ethindp/prism/), which has alleviated much of the screen reader headaches.

The mod hooks the game with [Byte Buddy](https://bytebuddy.net/), and the installer is adapted from the [Non-Visual Calculus](https://github.com/rashadnaqeeb/NonVisualCalculus) installer by Rashad Naqeeb.

Finally, thank you for playing and the bug reports; the mod would be worse off without your feedback.
