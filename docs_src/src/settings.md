# Settings

Ctrl+Shift+M opens the mod menu from anywhere. It holds:

- Settings
- Change log, which opens this mod's change log in your browser
- Latest release page, which opens it in your browser
- Close

Enter opens a page; Escape or Backspace goes back one page, and from the menu itself closes it.

## General

- Check for updates on launch: whether the mod checks the releases page when the game starts. On by default.

## What Is Spoken

Most of what the mod speaks is made of parts: a hero's row in a fight is its name, hp, shields, incoming damage and so on. You decide which parts are spoken, and in what order.

- **Announcements** has a page for every part, wherever it is spoken. Speak turns it off or on everywhere at once. Include suffix punctuation turns off the comma that follows it.
- **Readouts** are focus lines: what a control says when you land on it.
- **Buffers** are the buffers' lines; see [Buffers](buffers.md).
- **Glances** are the glance keys' lines (Ctrl+1 and its kind).
- **Events** are what is spoken as it happens: rolls, outcomes, banners, enemy intents.

Each readout, buffer, glance and event has its own page, with one row per part in the order they speak:

- the part's toggle, on or off;
- Move up and Move down, which move it in the order (focus stays on the button, so it can be pressed again);
- Configure, where the part has settings of its own for this readout.

A part's settings on a readout follow its global setting until you change them there. Reset to defaults, at the top of the page, puts the order and the parts back.

An event's page also has Announce, whether it is spoken at all, and Add to the log buffer, whether it is kept there.
