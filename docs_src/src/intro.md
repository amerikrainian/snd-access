# Introduction

SnD Access is a screen reader and keyboard accessibility mod for Slice & Dice, a dice-battler roguelike in which you lead a party of heroes through fights, rolling each hero's die and choosing what its side does.

## Features

- Speech output through Prism, with support for common screen readers and speech backends.
- Review buffers for the focused control, the hero or monster it concerns, their items, both sides of the fight, and a log of what happened.
- A context-sensitive key list (F1) that shows only the keys that do something where you are.
- Announcements you arrange yourself: turn parts of what is spoken off, or change their order, in the mod menu.
