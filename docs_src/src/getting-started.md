# Getting Started

## Basic Navigation

The basics are a screen reader's. Arrows move focus. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle control groups. Enter activates, as a click would. Backspace is the secondary action where a control has one, usually its details: a hero's character sheet, an item's text, whatever the game shows on a right-click. Escape backs out. Home and End jump to the ends of a list, Alt+Up and Alt+Down to the previous or next section. Typing letters searches the focused group by name.

Focus announcements are deliberately short: name, value, state, role. Everything longer a control carries, its description, keyword rules, a hero's six sides, waits in the buffers; see [Buffers](buffers.md).

## Keys Here

**Press F1, anywhere.** It lists the keys that do something right where you are: the game's own keys for the current phase of a fight (roll, lock dice, target, undo), the glances at the unit you are on, the buffer keys, and what Escape does on this screen. Enter on a row runs that key for you.

That list is the reference, and it is always current. [Controls](controls.md) is the full list for when you want to see everything at once.

## A Fight

Heroes and enemies share one control group, heroes on the left and enemies on the right: Left and Right cross between the sides, Up and Down move along one. When your turn opens, the mod reads what the enemies intend to do.

On a hero or a monster, Ctrl+1 reads its hp and shields, Ctrl+2 who it targets and who targets it, Ctrl+3 its statuses. Ctrl+I reads the damage coming at your whole party, Ctrl+T the turn, your rerolls and mana.

## The Mod Menu

Ctrl+Shift+M opens the mod menu from anywhere: the mod's settings, the change log, and the latest release page. See [Settings](settings.md).
